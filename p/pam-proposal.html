2

How it works

Every way someone finds you leads to the same place: a booked call on your calendar.

Someone raises a hand

Comments, replies to a story, DMs “CHALLENGE”, joins your list or visits the site.

Welcome AI replies

Within a minute, in your voice. Starts a friendly chat and saves their seat.

They're registered

Added to your CRM and tagged for the next challenge.

Follow up AI keeps them going

Day 1 to Day 4 links, reminders by email and text, replays for missed days.

They book a call

On Day 4 the follow up AI invites them to a 1:1 and books it on your calendar.

Good to know: Instagram doesn't let any tool message someone just because they followed you. So the welcome starts when a person comments, replies to a story, DMs a keyword or joins your list. Those are also the people most likely to sign up.
