About

I'm Yvette Kahn. I Build the Agents Myself.

Founder of Infinity Reach Media, out of Orange County. Twelve clients at a time, on purpose.

How This Started

I kept watching clients buy leads they already had.

Infinity Reach Media didn't start with ads. It started with organic content and follow-up systems that kept working after we logged off.

Then I noticed the same thing at almost every client. Customer lists worth thousands, sitting untouched. Calls ringing out while they did the actual work. And every one of them asking me to go buy more leads.

They didn't have a traffic problem. They had a follow-up problem. So I built the agents that go get that money back.

I've spent years writing marketing copy and building funnels. I don't have a computer science degree. I learned to build these because nobody was going to build them the way my clients actually needed them, and I got tired of waiting.

How We Operate

Six rules I won't bend on.

Not values. Rules. Every one of them costs us something, which is how you know we mean them.

🔑

Your data stays yours

We build inside your accounts, not a platform you have to rent access to. If you leave next month, you take everything with you. That's the point.

✍️

You approve the sends

Approval mode is on from day one. Your agent drafts, you tap approve. You can switch it off once you trust it, but that's your call to make.

📄

Promises go in the contract

Three guarantees, written word for word into our terms — not just on a landing page. Live in 7 days. 60-second lead response. 10 booked appointments.

🔢

Twelve clients. That's it.

Whoever scopes your build is whoever builds it and whoever picks up when something breaks. Capping the list is the only way that stays true.

⚙️

We built it, so we can fix it

These agents run on our own code, not a reseller kit with our logo on it. When you need something changed, we change it. We don't file a ticket with a vendor.

🚫

We'll tell you not to buy

If five people a month contact you, an agent won't save you. If your close rate is the problem, more appointments won't fix it. We say so on the call.

Who This Is For

Businesses that already have the customers.

The work only pays off if there's something to recover. Coaches whose best client started as a DM that sat for two days. Salons and med spas with a booking calendar full of gaps. Home services missing calls at 4:58 on a Friday. Insurance brokers sitting on a list they haven't touched in a year.

Every one of them was already earning that revenue. They were just losing it somewhere between the inquiry and the appointment.

If you're not sure that's you, don't take my word for it. Run your own numbers — it takes two minutes, it's free, and the report is yours whether we ever speak or not.

Bring me the hardest question you've got.

Thirty minutes, free, no deck. We run your real numbers together and you leave with a plan you could hand to somebody else entirely. If an agent isn't the answer for you, I'd rather say that on the call than sell you one.
