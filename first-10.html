Questions people actually ask

Won't reaching out to old customers annoy them?

It's the most common worry and it's almost always wrong. These people chose you once. A short, human message that sounds like you — not a blast — reads as good service, not spam. You approve the messaging before a single one sends, and anyone who asks to be left alone is removed immediately and permanently.

Will it sound like a robot?

The agents are trained on your business, your services, and how you actually talk to customers. Most people never realize they weren't texting your front desk. And you don't have to take our word for it — talk to a live one right now and try to stump it.

What if my list is messy?

Nearly all of them are. Duplicates, dead numbers, a spreadsheet somebody started three years ago. Cleaning and segmenting it is part of what you're paying for — hand it over however it exists.

How much work is this for me?

About fifteen minutes to hand over the list, then approving messaging before it goes out. That's the whole ask. We build it, we run it, you watch appointments appear.

What happens after the 30 days?

Entirely your call. Plenty of businesses run The First 10 and stop there — you keep the appointments and the revenue, no strings. If you'd rather keep it running and close the other leaks too, your $997 is credited toward your setup and we talk about the ongoing partnership. There's no automatic enrollment and no contract.

Why should I trust you with my customer list?

Your data lives in accounts you own, not ours. We never sell, share, or reuse it, and you can revoke our access at any moment and keep everything. That's written into our terms, not just promised on a page.
