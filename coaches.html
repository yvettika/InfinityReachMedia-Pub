Agent 01 · Speed to Lead

Every inquiry answered in 60 seconds

The leak: someone comments "info please" or DMs you at 10pm, fired up. You reply the next afternoon, between clients. The feeling is gone.

The agent replies instantly in your voice, answers the questions you always get, qualifies gently, and books the call straight into your calendar. You come off a session to a booked call, not a backlog.

Agent 02 · Show-Rate

The discovery calls that actually get held

The leak: booked is not the same as held. A no-show costs you the slot, the momentum, and the client you'd have enrolled.

The agent confirms, reminds at the right moments, and gives one-tap reschedule instead of a silent disappearance. You spend your call time on people who show up.

Agent 03 · Reactivation

The "not right now" list you never worked

The leak: everyone who inquired, took a call, or bought something small — and then life happened. That list is the warmest audience you will ever own.

The agent works it for you, in your voice, one conversation at a time, and books the ones whose timing finally changed. No ad spend, no new audience, no cold outreach.

Agent 04 · The Closer

"Let me think about it" doesn't have to end there

The leak: someone goes quiet mid-conversation. You don't want to chase, chasing feels desperate, and a week later the thread is cold.

The agent stays in the conversation and handles the moment honestly: it asks what specifically they'd want to think over — usually one answerable thing — offers to hold a slot they can cancel by text, and books the second they're ready. It never pesters, and it stops the moment someone asks it to.

Will it sound like me, or like a bot?

It's trained on your offer, your pricing rules, your qualifying questions, and your voice. It always says it's an AI assistant — in coaching, a prospect who feels tricked never books, so honesty is the setting. You approve every message until you trust it, and anything nuanced gets handed straight to you.

My list is small. Does this still work?

The speed-to-lead and no-show agents work at any size, because they act on people already talking to you. No. The agents sit on top of what you already run and feed it. They answer the inquiry, qualify, and book into your existing calendar. You keep your tools, and your data stays in your accounts — always.

No. It fits any coach or consultant who sells from a conversation — one-to-one, group, mastermind, or done-for-you. If a booked call is how you make money, the agents fit.
