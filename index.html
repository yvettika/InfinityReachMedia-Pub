Under the Hood

How Your Agent Learns Your Business

Fair question, and most agencies won't answer it. Your agent isn't a generic chatbot with your logo on it — it's our own sales engine, loaded with your business, and corrected by you.

📋

Your business is the source material

Onboarding loads your services, your real pricing rules, your service area and hours, your booking calendar, and the objections you already hear every week. Your existing customer list shows the agent how your buyers actually talk. Nothing gets guessed.

🎯

The selling method is ours, not the model's

Every agent runs the same written sales arc — open, qualify one question at a time, connect the value to what they just told you, then close on two specific times instead of "want to book?" — plus an objection playbook for price, "let me think about it," and comparison shopping. Real sales training, written down as rules.

🔍

You correct it, and it stays corrected

Approval mode is on by default, so you see messages before they send. Every conversation and booking is logged in your portal. When the agent gets a nuance wrong, we change the rule permanently. It always says it's an AI, it never invents a price, and it can't say "you're booked" unless it actually booked it.
