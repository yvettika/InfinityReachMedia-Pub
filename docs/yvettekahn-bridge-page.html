You don't need to be techy, famous, or a natural on camera. Here's the whole idea in plain English.

Questions I get a lot

Quick answers

Do I need a big following to make this work?

Nope. Camila started from scratch, and so do most people. What matters is sharing helpful content consistently. Small accounts make sales all the time when the content connects.

Do I have to create my own product?

You can, but you don't have to. Using a done for you digital product is one of the fastest ways to get started, because the product is ready and you can focus on sharing it.

I'm not techy. Is that a problem?

Not at all. The system is built to be beginner friendly, and the AI tools are plug and play. If you can post a video on Instagram, you can do this.

How much time does it take?

Most people start with an hour or so a day while they learn and set things up. Once it's running, it's mostly about posting consistently and letting the automation do its job.

How fast will I make money?

Honestly, it depends on you: how consistent you are, how much effort you put in, and a bit of timing too. Some people see a first sale quickly and others take a few months. Anyone who promises you a specific number by a specific date isn't being straight with you.
