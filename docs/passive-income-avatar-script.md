# Avatar Video Script: Passive Income Bridge Page

This is the video that plays at the top of `/passive-income`. Export it as an MP4 and save it as `videos/passive-income-avatar.mp4` (and a still frame as `images/passive-income-poster.jpg`). The page shows a "coming soon" panel until that file exists.

Numbers and years are spelled out the way they should be spoken, so your avatar's voice reads them naturally.

## Delivery notes for the avatar

* **Tone:** warm, friendly, like you're talking to a friend over coffee. Not salesy.
* **Pace:** a little slower than normal on the Camila numbers and on "one, two, three" so they land.
* **Smile moments:** the opening "I'm so glad you're here," the "that's when it clicked" line, and the closing.
* **On screen text ideas (optional, add in editing):**
  * "Less than $1,000" when you mention her move
  * "$3M+ in online sales, $0 in ads" on the big number
  * "1. Share  2. Sell  3. Automate" during the three steps
  * "Results not typical" small in the corner whenever income is mentioned
  * An arrow pointing down at the end: "Click the button below"
* **Background:** clean, bright home office or soft lavender to match the page.

## Before you publish: make it yours

1. **Your story has to be true to you.** The "I followed the steps... saw a sale I didn't have to chase" part is a draft. Swap in what really happened for you, like when you started and what your first win felt like. Real details always convert better than polished ones.
2. **If you add your own income number**, only use a real one you can back up with records, and keep the "results not typical" line on screen while you say it.
3. **Double check Camila's details** against her current site. The facts used here (moved to the U.S. in 2016 with under $1,000, worked as a nanny, found affiliate marketing in 2021, over $3 million in online sales with no paid ads) come from her public bio and interviews.
4. **Keep the affiliate mention.** If you earn a commission, a quick "I may earn a commission if you join through my link" in the post caption keeps you on the right side of FTC rules.
