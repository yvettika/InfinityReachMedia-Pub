## The 4 parts of every clone I build

1. **The face** (looks like you on video)
2. **The voice** (sounds like you, out loud and in writing)
3. **The scripts** (says what you'd actually say)
4. **The automation** (GoHighLevel posts it, catches the comments, replies, and books the call)

Most people stop at part 1 and wonder why nothing happens. Parts 3 and 4 are where the money is.

## Part 1: The face

### Step 1: Collect the right inputs

Before I open any tool, I send my client a checklist. Garbage in, garbage out, so this step matters more than the tool.

**The clone checklist:**

* 10 to 20 clear photos of you. Different angles, good light, face fully visible, no sunglasses, no heavy filters, no group shots.
* A few that show your normal look (the hair, glasses or style people know you by).
* 1 or 2 short videos of you talking to the camera, so I can see how you move and gesture.
* Your top topics, or the exact things you want the clone to say.
* The AI tool account (more on that below).

### Step 2: Pick your tool

I use two, depending on who it's for.

| | Google Gemini | Higgsfield |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Best for | Brand new, just testing | Real clients, regular content |
| How it works | Upload your picture, write a prompt for what you want it to do, and it makes the video | Train your likeness once, then generate as many images and videos as you want |
| Cost | Comes with Gemini | Starts around $47 a month for 1,200 credits |
| Catch | Only about 3 videos at a time, then you wait 24 hours | Every video costs credits (roughly 20 to 40 per video depending on length) |

I started on Gemini because it's the easiest way in. Upload a photo, describe the scene, done. But the 3 videos a day limit makes it a slow process once you're serious.

For client work I use **Higgsfield**. It's faster and the quality is better. Inside it you can train what they call a **Soul character**: you upload 5 to 20 photos of the same person, it trains in about 10 minutes, and from then on every image and video keeps the same face. Inside Higgsfield you also get access to a bunch of models (Nano Banana, Kling, Seedream and more). In my testing, **Seedream 4.5** has given me the most realistic results.

💡 **Pro tip on accounts:** have your client buy the Higgsfield plan in **their** name and share the login with you through a password manager. Credits are their cost, not yours, and they own the clone if you ever part ways. It also makes your pricing way simpler.

### Step 3: Try the hybrid trick

One of my most asked about videos was me walking through a door into a gorgeous background. People thought it was my clone. It wasn't. It was **a real video of me, with an AI background built around it.**

That hybrid approach is my favorite for anything personal. Your real body language, your real face, AI doing the heavy lifting on the set. Nobody can tell what's real, and you didn't need a studio.

### Step 4: Get likeness feedback (and expect it)

Here's something nobody tells you. **People who've met you in person will be pickier than people who only know you through a screen.** My clone fools people who know me from video. People who know me in real life can tell.

So when your client says "that doesn't look like me" (mine did!), that's normal. It's not failure, it's round two. Ask what feels off (smile, hair, face shape, age), swap in better photos, and regenerate. Usually it takes a couple of rounds.

## Part 2: The voice

There are really two voices: how you **sound** and how you **write**.

### The spoken voice

Clone your voice with a tool like ElevenLabs (it takes about a minute of clean audio to start). Record in a quiet room, talk the way you normally talk, not your "presenting" voice. Then use that same voice on **every** clone video so your audience hears one consistent you.

### The written voice (this is the one most people skip)

Your clone also has to write captions, DMs and follow ups that sound like you. Here's the exact loop I use:

1. Pull your **10 best posts** and **10 real replies you've sent to leads**. Real ones, typos and all.
2. Paste them into your AI tool and write: *"This is my voice. Study the tone, rhythm, and words I use."*
3. Have it draft your next post. Edit only what feels off. **Feed those edits back.**

That loop is how your clone gets smarter every single week. The one mistake that makes AI content sound generic? Training it on what you *think* you sound like (your About page, your bio) instead of what you *actually* sound like (your DMs).

## Part 3: The scripts

A beautiful clone saying nothing useful is just an expensive screensaver.

How I do scripts:

* **Write a couple of versions of each video.** Don't overthink it. Post two, see which performs, and let your client pick the one that sounds most like them. (Mine had a clear favorite after the first batch.)
* **Hook in the first 2 seconds.** A contrarian or "wait, what?" line. Example: *"Everyone is using AI to write posts. Almost nobody is using it to answer the DM at 11pm."*
* **One message per video.** One idea, one story, one point.
* **One call to action.** Always a comment keyword (more on why in Part 4). Example: *"Comment CLONE and I'll send you the setup."*
* **Every video points to a real human moment.** A call, a roundtable, a workshop. That's where the trust gets built.

### Let's talk about the "it's not authentic" objection

I hear it all the time: "Won't people think it's fake? It doesn't feel like the real me."

Two things:

1. **Be upfront.** Instagram and Facebook are labeling AI content now. Disclose it. Say it's your AI clone in the caption. Hiding it is what kills trust, not the clone itself.
2. **The clone isn't the closer. You are.** If the message is good, people watch it, just like they watch an ad (and most ads have AI in them now). Then the clone sends them to a call with the real you. *There's* your authenticity.

## Part 4: The automation in GoHighLevel

This is where the clone stops being content and starts being a lead machine. Here's the exact setup.

### Step 1: Connect your socials to Social Planner

**Marketing → Social Planner → connect** Instagram, Facebook, and anything else you post to. Now you can upload a batch of clone videos once and schedule a whole month.

My setup: one batch day, then the posts go out on their own. I even have a reel that auto posts every Monday at 8am. You never touch your phone to post again.

**Every caption gets two things:** the AI disclosure line, and the comment keyword.

### Step 2: Comment keyword → DM workflow

**Automation → Workflows → Create Workflow** (start from scratch). I call it "Clone: Comment to DM."

* **Trigger:** Instagram comment on a post (add Facebook comments too if you post there). Filter: comment contains your keyword, like `CLONE`.
* **Action:** Send an Instagram DM. Keep it short, friendly, and in your voice: thank them, give them the thing you promised, ask one question.
* **Action:** Add tag `clone-lead`.
* **Action:** Create/Update Opportunity in your clone pipeline (see Step 4), stage **New Lead**.

Save, then **Publish**. (I can't tell you how many times I "built" a workflow and forgot to hit Publish. 🙃)

### Step 3: Let your AI reply in your voice, gated by a tag

When someone replies to that DM, you want an instant answer that sounds like you, not "Thanks for your message!"

* **Trigger:** Customer Replied (leave the channel open so it catches DMs, texts, email, web chat).
* **Filter: Has tag = `clone-lead`.** 🚨 **Do not skip this.** Without it, your AI answers *every* reply in your account. Your clients mid conversation. Your vendors. Your mom.
* **Action:** hand the conversation to your AI. If you're starting out, GoHighLevel's built in **Conversation AI** works great here. Train it with the same 10 real replies from Part 2, your FAQs, and one rule: *never quote a price, always offer a call.* (I run mine through a custom brain I built with Claude, using a webhook action, but you don't need that to start.)

**Two hard rules I never break:**
* The AI always says it's an AI if anyone asks. Always.
* The AI stops at the booking. A human runs every call.

### Step 4: A pipeline so you can see if it's working

**Opportunities → Pipelines → Create.** Mine looks like this:

**New Lead → Qualified → Call Booked → Showed / No Show → Won / Lost**

* The comment workflow drops people into **New Lead**.
* An **Appointment Booked** workflow moves them to **Call Booked** automatically and sends the confirmation and reminders.
* You move **Showed, Won and Lost** yourself after the call, and put the deal value on Won.

Now you can answer the only question that matters: *"How many calls did my clone book this month, and how much did they close for?"*

### Step 5: Outcome tags

Add these so nothing slips through the cracks:

* `ai-booked`: booked a call
* `ai-handoff`: needs a human, and it triggers an internal notification to you
* `ai-opted-out`: said STOP, never contact again

### Step 6: SMS consent on every form (do this today)

Humbling moment: when I checked my own account, only **4 of 284** contacts had SMS consent on file, even though 157 had phone numbers. That means I legally couldn't text most of my own list.

Add an unchecked SMS consent checkbox to every form and map it to a contact field. Future you will thank you.

## One rule that will save you a headache

**One bot per inbox.** If you're using ManyChat for Instagram DMs, don't also let GoHighLevel's AI answer those same DMs. Two bots fighting over one person is a terrible first impression. Pick one.
