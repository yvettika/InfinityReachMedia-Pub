**🎯 The play: my AI clone posts the video, GoHighLevel books the call**

**What I did:** trained an AI clone in Higgsfield, scripted it from my real DMs, and scheduled the videos in GHL Social Planner with a comment keyword. Two GHL workflows do the rest:

1️⃣ **Comment → DM → Pipeline.** Trigger: Instagram comment contains CLONE. Actions: send DM, add tag `clone-lead`, create opportunity in "Clone Leads" at New Lead.

2️⃣ **AI replies in my voice.** Trigger: Customer Replied. **Filter: Has tag `clone-lead`** (skip this and your AI answers your whole account 😬). Conversation AI, trained on 10 of my real DM replies, books the call. I take the call.

**Why it matters:** every conversation starts because someone raised their hand on a post. Zero cold DMs, zero videos filmed, and I only show up for the call.

**The exact DM:**
> Hey {{first name}}! Saw your CLONE comment 🙌 Here's the full setup I use: [link]. Quick question so I can point you to the right part: are you building a clone for your own content, or for clients?

**Numbers:** 5 to 20 photos to train the clone. Higgsfield starts around $47/mo for 1,200 credits, and a video runs about 20 to 40 credits. 2 workflows.

**What didn't work:**
* Gemini for client clones. Fine to test, but only about 3 videos before a 24 hour wait.
* Round one of my client's clone: "That doesn't look like me." Normal. Swap photos, regenerate.
* Building a workflow and forgetting to hit Publish. 🙃
* Only 4 of my 284 GHL contacts had SMS consent. Fix your forms before you need them.
