The AI Clone Playbook

One play: an AI clone of you posts the video, GoHighLevel catches the comment, DMs them, drops them in the pipeline, and an AI that writes like you books the call. You only show up for the call.

Yvette Kahn · Infinity Reach Media

  1. 1 · CloneYour AI clone posts the video
  2. 2 · CommentThey comment your keyword
  3. 3 · DMGHL DMs them in seconds
  4. 4 · ReplyAI answers in your voice
  5. 5 · BookThe real you takes the call

What I did. I trained an AI clone in Higgsfield, scripted it from my real DMs, and scheduled the videos in GoHighLevel Social Planner with a comment keyword. One workflow DMs every commenter, tags them, and drops them into a pipeline. A second workflow, gated by that tag, lets an AI trained on my real replies answer and book the call.

Why it matters. Booking calls from DMs without spamming strangers: every conversation starts because they raised their hand on a post. Nobody gets a cold message.

The play, step by step

1

Build the clone (Higgsfield, not Gemini)

Train a Soul character in Higgsfield with 5 to 20 photos of the same person. It takes about 10 minutes, and from then on the face stays consistent across every video. Of the models inside Higgsfield, Seedream 4.5 has given me the most realistic results.

Take home · The clone checklist I send clients
Before I build your clone, send me:
1. 10 to 20 clear photos of you. Different angles, good light, face fully visible. No sunglasses, no filters, no group shots.
2. A few in your "normal" look (the hair, glasses or style people know you by).
3. 1 or 2 short videos of you talking to the camera, so the clone moves like you.
4. Your top 5 topics, or the exact lines you want it to say.
5. A Higgsfield account in your name, login shared through a password manager.

Account tip: the client buys the plan in their own name. Credits are their cost, they own the clone, and your pricing gets simpler.

My favorite trick: for anything personal, shoot a real clip of yourself (I walked through a door) and let AI build the set around you. Real body language, AI background. People still ask me if that one was the clone.

2

Script it from your real DMs, not your bio

Generic AI content comes from training on who you think you are (your About page). Train it on who you actually are: your posts and the replies you've sent real leads. Then run the edit loop every week.

Take home · The voice prompt
Below are my 10 best posts and 10 real replies I've sent to leads. This is my voice. Study the tone, rhythm, and words I use.

Write 2 versions of a 30 second video script about [TOPIC].
Rules:
• Hook in the first 2 seconds. Contrarian or "wait, what?"
• One idea only.
• Sound like the examples, not like a marketer.
• End with: "Comment [KEYWORD] and I'll send you [THE THING]."

[PASTE 10 REAL DM REPLIES]

The loop: edit only what feels off, then paste your edits back with "here's what I changed and why." Post both versions and let your client (or the numbers) pick the winner.

3

Schedule it in Social Planner with a keyword

Marketing → Social Planner. Connect Instagram and Facebook, batch upload a month of clone videos, schedule. Every caption ends with the same two lines.

Caption ending
Comment CLONE and I'll send you the setup. 👇

(Made with my AI clone. The ideas and the DMs are all me.)

Disclose it. Instagram and Facebook are labeling AI content anyway. Hiding it is what kills trust, not the clone.

Why the question: it's one a person can answer in three words, and any reply is a live conversation. That reply is what fires Workflow 2.

5

Workflow 2: AI replies in your voice and books the call

Trigger Customer Replied, channel left open so it catches DMs, texts, email and web chat. Then hand the thread to GoHighLevel's Conversation AI (or your own AI by webhook, which is how I run mine with Claude).

Filter: Has tag = clone-lead. Do not skip this. Without it your AI answers every reply in your whole account. Your clients mid conversation. Your vendors. Your mom.
Take home · The AI reply prompt
You are [NAME]'s AI assistant. You only talk to people who commented on [NAME]'s posts or messaged first.

How to talk: short, warm, casual, one question at a time. Match the examples below. No marketing speak.

Your goal: understand what they're working on and what's in the way, then offer two specific times for a call with [NAME] from the calendar. Never ask "want to book?" Offer the times.

Rules you never break:
• If anyone asks if you're a real person, say you're [NAME]'s AI assistant and offer to have [NAME] reply personally.
• Never quote a price. Say [NAME] covers that on the call.
• You stop at the booking. [NAME] runs every call.
• If someone says stop, stop.

6

A pipeline so you can see if it's working

Opportunities → Pipelines → Create "Clone Leads":

New Lead → Qualified → Call Booked → Showed / No Show → Won / Lost

Workflow 1 drops people in New Lead. An Appointment Booked workflow moves them to Call Booked and sends reminders. You move Showed, Won and Lost after the call, and put the deal value on Won. Add three tags: ai-booked, ai-handoff (pings you), ai-opted-out.

The one number that matters: calls booked from clone videos this month, and what they closed for.

What didn't work (so you can skip it)

  • Starting clients on Gemini.Great for a free first test, but you only get about 3 videos, then a 24 hour wait. I moved client work to Higgsfield.
  • Canceling my Higgsfield plan because I "wasn't making that many."Then a client clone came in and I needed it back. Have each client buy their own plan.
  • "That doesn't look like me." My client said it after round one.Normal. People are pickiest about their own face, and anyone who knows you in person is pickier than people who know you on camera. Ask what feels off (smile, hair, age), swap photos, regenerate.
  • "It doesn't feel authentic." The objection I hear most.Disclose it, and remember the clone isn't the closer. It sends people to a call with the real you. That's where the authenticity lives.
  • Building a workflow and never hitting Publish.More than once. 🙃 Save, Publish, then comment your own keyword from a second account and check Execution Logs.
  • Only 4 of my 284 GHL contacts had SMS consent (157 had phone numbers).I couldn't legally text most of my own list. Add an unchecked SMS consent box to every form and map it to a contact field.
  • Two bots on one inbox.If ManyChat runs your Instagram DMs, don't also tag those contacts for GHL's AI. Pick one per inbox.
