# Loom Script: The AI Clone Playbook (about 5 minutes)

**Format:** Loom, screen + face bubble. Lead with the goods, one play, show the real screens.
**Goes with:** the page at /members/ai-clone-playbook and the Skool post in `ai-clone-ghl-lesson.md`.

**Open before you hit record:**
* A clone video ready to play
* Higgsfield (Soul character screen)
* GHL: Social Planner, the two workflows, the Clone Leads pipeline
* Your phone with a second Instagram account, logged in

**Delivery:** talk to one person. Real beats polished, especially in a video about clones.

---

## 0:00 · The play (play 3 seconds of a clone video, then you)

> That was my AI clone. Here's the play in one sentence: my clone posts the video, someone comments a keyword, GoHighLevel DMs them, an AI that writes like me books the call, and I only show up for the call. Let me show you exactly how it's wired.

## 0:20 · The clone (screen: Higgsfield)

**ON SCREEN:** Soul character training, then the model picker.

> I train a Soul character in Higgsfield with five to twenty photos. Takes about ten minutes, and the face stays the same in every video. Seedream 4.5 has been the most realistic for me. The client buys the plan in their name, about forty seven a month for twelve hundred credits, and a video runs twenty to forty credits.
>
> Quick miss: I started clients on Gemini. Fine for a test, but you get about three videos, then a twenty four hour wait. Too slow.

## 1:00 · The scripts (screen: AI tool with the voice prompt)

> The scripts come from my real DMs, not my bio. I paste my ten best posts and ten real replies to leads, and tell it: this is my voice, study the tone, rhythm and words. Two versions per video, a hook in the first two seconds, and every one ends with "comment CLONE."

## 1:30 · Social Planner (screen: GHL Social Planner)

> I batch a month in Social Planner. Every caption ends with the keyword and one honest line: made with my AI clone, the ideas and the DMs are all me.

## 1:50 · Workflow 1 (screen: the workflow, click each step)

> Workflow one. Trigger: Instagram comment contains CLONE. Action: send this DM.

**ON SCREEN:** Zoom in on the DM copy.

> It gives them the link, then asks one easy question: building a clone for your own content, or for clients? Any answer starts a real conversation.
>
> Then add the tag clone lead, and create the opportunity in my Clone Leads pipeline. Save, and Publish. Yes, I've forgotten Publish. More than once.

## 2:40 · Workflow 2 (screen: the trigger filter)

> Workflow two. Trigger: Customer Replied. And this filter is the whole game: has tag clone lead. Skip it and your AI answers every reply in your account. Your clients. Your mom.
>
> Then Conversation AI takes over, trained on my real replies, with four rules: admit it's AI if asked, never quote a price, offer two real times, stop at the booking. I run every call.

## 3:20 · Live test (screen: phone, then GHL)

**ON SCREEN:** Comment CLONE from your second account. Show the DM arriving, then the card in New Lead.

> Let's test it. Commenting CLONE... there's the DM. And there's the card in New Lead.

*(If it doesn't fire, keep recording. Open Execution Logs and fix it on camera. That's the most useful minute of the video.)*

## 3:50 · What else didn't work (on camera)

> Two more misses so you skip them. My client's first reaction was "that doesn't look like me." Totally normal. Swap photos, regenerate. And when I checked my GHL, only four of two hundred eighty four contacts had SMS consent. Fix your forms now.

## 4:15 · Take home (on camera)

> Everything I just showed you is on the page linked under this video. The photo checklist, the voice prompt, the exact DM, the AI reply prompt, every workflow setting, all with copy buttons.
>
> Your move this week: build workflow one, comment your own keyword from a second account, and post the screenshot in the thread. I want to see it.
