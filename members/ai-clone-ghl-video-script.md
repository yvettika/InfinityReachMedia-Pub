# Video Script: How I Build AI Clones + the GoHighLevel Automation

**Format:** screen share + face cam (Zoom, Loom or OBS). Talking head on camera, screen when you're clicking.
**Length:** about 14 to 16 minutes.
**Goes with:** the text lesson (`ai-clone-ghl-lesson.md`). Same order, so members can follow along.

**Have open before you hit record:**
* A finished clone video (a client's, with permission, or your own) ready to play
* Your hybrid "walk through the door" video
* Higgsfield, logged in (Soul character screen)
* Gemini, logged in
* ChatGPT or Claude with your 10 posts + 10 DM replies ready to paste
* GoHighLevel: Social Planner, Workflows, Opportunities
* A second Instagram account on your phone to comment the keyword live

**Delivery notes:** talk like you're on a call with one member, not presenting to a room. It's okay to say "um." Real beats polished, especially in a video about AI clones.

---

## 0:00 · Cold open (on camera, then play the clone clip)

**ON SCREEN:** Play 5 to 8 seconds of a clone video. Then cut to you, live.

**SAY:**
> Okay. Was that me, or was that my clone?
>
> (pause)
>
> Honestly? Some people who've met me in person can tell. People who only know me from video usually can't. And that little difference is actually one of the most important things I'm going to teach you today.
>
> This month's zone of genius is how I build AI clones, and more importantly, how I plug them into GoHighLevel so they don't just make content. They bring in leads and book calls while I'm doing literally anything else.

---

## 0:45 · Why this matters (on camera)

**SAY:**
> Real talk. You answer every DM. You write every post. You film every video. And the second you log off, your business goes quiet.
>
> A coach I'm building a clone for told me on our first call, "I don't want to make my videos anymore. I want it to do the YouTube and all that." His goal isn't views. It's filling his weekly roundtables with ten to fifteen people without hiring a VA.
>
> That's the real job of a clone. Not a party trick. A version of you that keeps showing up and keeps sending people to the place where the real you closes.
>
> Every clone I build has four parts: the face, the voice, the scripts, and the automation. Most people stop at the face and wonder why nothing happens. The money is in parts three and four. Let's go.

**ON SCREEN (lower third or quick slide):** Face · Voice · Scripts · Automation

---

## 1:45 · Part 1, the face: inputs (screen: the checklist)

**ON SCREEN:** The clone checklist from the text lesson.

**SAY:**
> Before I open any tool, I send my client this checklist. Garbage in, garbage out.
>
> Ten to twenty clear photos. Different angles, good light, no sunglasses, no filters, no group shots. Include your normal look, the hair or glasses people know you by. One or two short videos of you talking to camera so the AI can learn how you move. And your topics, or the exact things you want it to say.

---

## 2:45 · Part 1: Gemini vs Higgsfield (screen: both tools)

**ON SCREEN:** Gemini first. Upload a photo, type a short prompt, show the result.

**SAY:**
> If you're brand new, start here in Gemini. Upload your picture, tell it what you want, and it makes the video. Super easy.
>
> The catch is you only get about three videos at a time, then you wait twenty four hours. Fine for testing. Painful once you're serious.

**ON SCREEN:** Switch to Higgsfield. Show the Soul character training screen and the model picker.

**SAY:**
> For clients, I use Higgsfield. It's faster and the quality is better. You train what they call a Soul character. Five to twenty photos of the same person, about ten minutes, and from then on the face stays consistent in every image and video.
>
> You get a bunch of models in here. Nano Banana, Kling, Seedream. In my testing Seedream 4.5 has been the most realistic.
>
> Pricing starts around forty seven a month for twelve hundred credits, and a video runs roughly twenty to forty credits depending on length.
>
> Big tip: have your client buy the plan in their own name and share the login with you through a password manager. Credits are their cost, and they own the clone.

## 4:30 · Part 1: the hybrid trick (play the door video)

**ON SCREEN:** Play your walk through the door video.

**SAY:**
> This one gets asked about constantly. Everybody thought this was my clone. It wasn't. It's a real video of me, with an AI background built around it.
>
> For anything personal, this is my favorite. Your real body language, your real face, and the AI builds the set. No studio needed.

## 5:15 · Part 1: likeness feedback (on camera)

**SAY:**
> Now, here's what nobody tells you. When you show your client their clone, there's a good chance they'll say "that doesn't look like me." Mine did.
>
> That's normal. It's round two, not failure. People are way pickier about their own face, and people who know you in person are pickier than people who know you through a screen.
>
> So ask what feels off. The smile? The hair? It looks older? Swap in better photos and regenerate. Usually takes a couple of rounds.

---

## 6:00 · Part 2, the voice (screen: AI tool)

**SAY:**
> There are two voices. How you sound and how you write.
>
> For how you sound, clone your voice with something like ElevenLabs. Record in a quiet room and talk like you actually talk, not your presenting voice. Use that same voice on every clone video.
>
> But the one everybody skips is the written voice. Watch this.

**ON SCREEN:** Paste 10 posts + 10 real DM replies. Type the prompt live.

**SAY:**
> I pull my ten best posts and ten real replies I've sent to leads. Real ones, typos and all. Then I write: "This is my voice. Study the tone, rhythm, and words I use."
>
> Now I have it draft my next post.

**ON SCREEN:** Generate. Edit one or two lines out loud.

**SAY:**
> I only edit what feels off. And then I feed those edits back. That loop is how your clone gets smarter every week.
>
> The mistake that makes AI sound generic? Training it on your bio or your About page. That's who you think you are. Your DMs are who you actually are.

---

## 7:45 · Part 3, the scripts (on camera)

**SAY:**
> A gorgeous clone saying nothing useful is just an expensive screensaver.
>
> Here's how I write scripts. I do a couple of versions of each video, post two, and let the numbers and my client pick. Mine had a clear favorite after the first batch.
>
> Hook in the first two seconds. Something like, "Everyone is using AI to write posts. Almost nobody is using it to answer the DM at 11pm."
>
> One message per video. And one call to action, always a comment keyword. You'll see why in a second.

---

## 8:30 · The authenticity objection (on camera)

**SAY:**
> Okay, the elephant in the room. "Won't people think it's fake?"
>
> Two things. First, be upfront. Instagram and Facebook are labeling AI content now anyway. Say it's your AI clone in the caption. Hiding it is what kills trust, not the clone.
>
> Second, the clone isn't the closer. You are. If the message is good, people watch it, same as an ad. Then the clone sends them to a call with the real you. That's where the authenticity lives.

---

## 9:15 · Part 4, GoHighLevel: Social Planner (screen: GHL)

**ON SCREEN:** Marketing → Social Planner. Show connected accounts and a scheduled clone post.

**SAY:**
> Now the fun part. This is where the clone turns into a lead machine.
>
> First, Social Planner. Connect your Instagram, Facebook, whatever you post to. Then you batch upload your clone videos once and schedule the whole month. I have a reel that goes out every Monday at 8am without me touching anything.
>
> Every caption gets two things: the AI disclosure, and the comment keyword.

---

## 10:00 · Comment to DM workflow (screen: Workflows)

**ON SCREEN:** Automation → Workflows → Create Workflow. Build it live.

**SAY:**
> Workflow number one. I call it "Clone: Comment to DM."
>
> Trigger is Instagram comment on a post, and I filter it to comments that contain CLONE.
>
> Action one, send an Instagram DM. Short, friendly, sounds like me. Thank them, give them what I promised, ask one question.
>
> Action two, add the tag clone dash lead.
>
> Action three, create the opportunity in my pipeline, stage New Lead.
>
> Save. And then, the step everyone forgets, Publish.

---

## 11:15 · AI replies, gated by the tag (screen: Workflows)

**ON SCREEN:** New workflow. Trigger Customer Replied. Zoom in on the tag filter.

**SAY:**
> Workflow number two. When they reply to that DM, I want an instant answer in my voice.
>
> Trigger is Customer Replied. And look at this filter. Has tag, clone dash lead.
>
> Do not skip this. Without it, your AI answers every single reply in your account. Your clients. Your vendors. Your mom.
>
> Then I hand the conversation to the AI. If you're starting out, GoHighLevel's Conversation AI works great. Train it with those same ten real replies, your FAQs, and one rule: never quote a price, always offer a call.
>
> And two rules I never break. The AI always admits it's an AI if anyone asks. And it stops at the booking. A human runs every call.

---

## 12:45 · Pipeline + tags (screen: Opportunities)

**ON SCREEN:** Opportunities → Pipelines. Show the stages. Then a contact with outcome tags.

**SAY:**
> Now, how do you know if any of this is working? The pipeline.
>
> New Lead, Qualified, Call Booked, Showed or No Show, Won or Lost.
>
> The comment workflow drops people in New Lead. An Appointment Booked workflow moves them to Call Booked and sends reminders. I move Showed, Won and Lost myself after the call.
>
> Plus three tags: ai booked, ai handoff which pings me, and ai opted out for anyone who says STOP.
>
> Now I can answer the only question that matters. How many calls did my clone book this month, and what did they close for?

**SAY:**

*(If it doesn't fire on the first try, keep it in! Show them the Execution Logs in the workflow and fix it live. That's the most valuable minute in the video.)*

## 14:30 · Two warnings + recap (on camera)

**SAY:**
> Two quick warnings before you go build.
>
> One bot per inbox. If ManyChat runs your Instagram DMs, don't let GoHighLevel's AI answer them too. Two bots fighting over one person is a terrible first impression.
>
> And put an SMS consent checkbox on every form. When I checked my own account, only four out of two hundred eighty four contacts had consent on file. I couldn't legally text most of my own list.
>
> So here's the whole flow. Clone video posts. Someone comments. GoHighLevel DMs them and drops them in the pipeline. Your AI answers in your voice and books the call. And the real you shows up and closes.
