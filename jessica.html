Common questions

Straight answers

Does Jessica pretend to be a person?

Never. Her first line names your business and says she's an AI assistant. If someone asks, she tells the truth and offers a human. That's hardcoded. Not a setting we or a client can turn off.

Does she run the sales call?

No. She works the conversation up to a booked appointment. A human owns the deal from the moment it's on the calendar. That's our rule for every client, including us.

Which channels does she cover?

Text, phone, Instagram DMs, Facebook Messenger, email, WhatsApp and website chat. It's one conversation per person across all of them, so nobody repeats themselves.

Does she send cold messages?

No. Jessica only replies to people who contacted the business first or gave written consent. No purchased lists, no blasts. Anyone who texts STOP never hears from her again.

Will she sound like my business or like a bot?

She's trained on your services, your pricing rules, your qualifying questions and your FAQs. You can name her whatever fits your brand. You approve messages until you trust her, and anything she shouldn't handle gets handed to a person with a note.

What does it cost?

We price it on the call, on the leak she plugs, not on the software. If you'd rather have your numbers before that conversation, run the scorecard first.
