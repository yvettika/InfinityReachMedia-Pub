AI Receptionist

5–7 business days Your time: about 7 min

We build: a phone receptionist that answers in your business name, answers your common questions, and books, moves or cancels appointments. You keep your phone number. We set up a new line for the AI, and your calls forward to it.

Your one jobTurn on call forwarding from your business phone. On test day we text you the exact code for your carrier. Usually you dial it once and you're done (about 1 minute). To turn it off, dial *73 on most carriers.
Which calls go to the AI? You choose
  • Missed calls only (most popular): your team answers when they can. If nobody picks up after about 4 rings, the AI answers.
  • After hours: the AI answers only when you're closed.
  • All calls: the AI answers everything. Good if you have no front desk.

On an office phone system (RingCentral, Ooma, Spectrum, Comcast)? It's a setting in your provider's online account. We'll walk you through it on a 5-minute call.

Dead List Miner

3–5 business days Your time: about 15 min

We build: a personal win-back campaign, written in your voice, to past customers and old leads who already know you. Before anything sends, we check your list and tell you who's safe to email. Replies go straight to your inbox.

Your one jobExport your customer list as a spreadsheet and send it to us. Then reply YES to the short email where we confirm the address your campaign will send from.
How to export your list
  • Columns we need: name and email. Nice to have: phone and last visit or purchase date. The date helps us email your most recent customers first.
  • Most booking, CRM and point-of-sale tools have an "Export" or "Download CSV" button on the customers or contacts page.
  • Send it by replying to your welcome email with the file attached. Please don't paste it into this form.
  • Only your own customers and people who contacted you. We can't email bought or scraped lists. No sending setup makes those safe.

Review Machine

3–5 business days Your time: about 5 min

We build: review requests to your recent customers, all pointing to your public Google review page, plus drafted replies to new reviews for you to approve. Every customer gets the same link. We never filter out unhappy customers, which is what keeps it within Google's rules.

Your one jobPaste your Google review link into the form, and send a list of recent customers. If you're also using Dead List Miner, the same spreadsheet works.
Where to find your Google review link
  1. Search your business name on Google while signed in to the account that manages your Business Profile.
  2. Click Ask for reviews (sometimes under "Read reviews" or "Share review form").
  3. Copy the link and paste it into the form. Can't find it? Leave it blank and we'll find it for you.
