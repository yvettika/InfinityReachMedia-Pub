Accessibility Statement

Last updated: September 11, 2026 · Infinity Reach Media, Orange County, California

Our commitment

Infinity Reach Media is working to make infinityreachmedia.com usable by as many people as possible, including people who browse with a screen reader, navigate by keyboard alone, use voice control, or rely on magnification or high-contrast settings. We treat accessibility as part of building the site, not a box to tick afterwards.

The standard we work to

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA. These are the guidelines referenced by the Americans with Disabilities Act and by the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services for the health-sector deadline in May 2027.

What we have done

  • Body text meets or exceeds the 4.5:1 contrast minimum against its background, and most of the site now exceeds the stricter 7:1 AAA threshold.
  • Every form field has a label programmatically associated with it, required fields are marked with a visible asterisk and announced as required, and validation errors name the specific field and what to correct.
  • The site can be operated by keyboard alone, with a visible focus indicator and a skip link to jump past the navigation.
  • Navigation collapses to a labelled menu button on small screens, and that button reports whether the menu is open.
  • Pages use real landmarks and a single descriptive heading order so assistive technology can navigate structurally.
  • Images carry alternative text, and decorative images are hidden from assistive technology.

Known limitations

We would rather name these than imply the site is perfect:

  • Some pages embed third-party tools — a Calendly scheduler and a live chat agent. We do not control their markup and cannot guarantee they meet AA. If one of them blocks you, email or text us and we will complete the same task with you directly.
  • Video testimonials on our reviews page are hosted on third-party players. Captions depend on that platform.
  • We are continuing to audit older landing pages and fix issues as we find them.

Tell us about a barrier

If any part of this site stops you from doing what you came to do, we want to know, and we will help you complete it another way in the meantime.

  • Email: info@infinityreachmedia.com
  • Text or call: (714) 924-3024

We aim to reply within one business day. Please tell us the page, what you were trying to do, and the assistive technology or browser you were using, and we will reproduce it from there.

Formal approach

This statement reflects our current, good-faith assessment of the site. It is not a legal guarantee of conformance. We review it when the site changes materially and at least once a year.
