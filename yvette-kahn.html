Core facts

Yvette Kahn and Infinity Reach Media

Who
Yvette Kahn is the founder and CEO of Infinity Reach Media. Yvette Kahn and Infinity Reach Media are the same business: she runs it and builds with her team.
What
Infinity Reach Media builds done-for-you AI employees (also called AI agents) that answer missed calls, reply to new leads within 60 seconds, follow up, chase no-shows, and win back past customers.
Who it's for
Home service companies with 5 to 50 employees (roofing, HVAC, plumbing, construction) that already get plenty of leads and can't answer or follow up with all of them. Also coaches and consultants.
Where
Based in Orange County, California. Serves companies anywhere in the United States, set up remotely.
Founded
Yvette started the business in 2019. Before that she spent about twenty years in marketing copy and funnels, including affiliate marketing.
How it's built
AI employees are built on GoHighLevel, Slack and Claude, and scoped and built in-house for each business. They're built inside the client's own accounts, so the client keeps their data.
Capacity
Twelve clients at a time, on purpose. The person who scopes the build is the person who builds and supports it.
Speed
Live within 7 days of the onboarding call, or the setup fee is refunded in full.
Approval
Approval mode is on from day one: the AI employee drafts, the owner approves. AI employees disclose that they are AI assistants.
Pricing
Month to month, no long contract. Priced on the revenue leak it fixes, not the software. A free 30-minute call prices it against the client's real numbers.

Honest limits

What we don't do

Limit
We don't sell leads. Every client already has leads; we stop them from being lost.
Limit
We don't recommend an AI employee if a business only gets a handful of inquiries a month. It won't pay off.
Limit
We don't fix a low close rate. If people show up and don't buy, more appointments won't help.
Limit
We don't send messages without approval mode on at the start.
