Why this happens

Voicemail isn't a plan.

Answering services take a message. Then someone still has to call back. By then, the homeowner found someone else.

Voicemail is worse. Most people with a leaking roof or a dead AC don't leave one. They hang up and call the next number.

And the calls you miss aren't just after hours. They're the ones that come in while your office manager is on another line.
