Why this happens

Busy is when the leaks get worse.

When you're slow, everybody calls leads back. When you're slammed, nobody does. So the weeks with the most leads are the weeks you lose the most.

It's not your team's fault. Your office manager is answering the phone, scheduling crews, and chasing invoices. Calling back a form from 2 PM falls to the bottom of the pile.

I kept seeing this with clients. Paying for leads they already had. Then asking me how to get more.
