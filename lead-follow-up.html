The short answer

How fast should you follow up with a lead?

How fast
Within 5 minutes, ideally within 1. The MIT / InsideSales Lead Response Management study found leads contacted within 5 minutes were far more likely to be qualified than leads contacted at 30.
How many times
More than once. Set a schedule: right away, later that day, the next day, then spaced out over the next couple of weeks.
Which channel
Text first. Most homeowners will answer a text before they pick up an unknown number.
What to say
Ask one easy question. Not 'just checking in.' Something they can answer in a few words.
When to stop
When they book, say no, or ask you to stop. Never keep texting after that.
How to make it automatic
An AI employee built on GoHighLevel, Slack and Claude, and scoped and built in-house for each business. It runs the whole schedule for every lead.

Common questions

Straight answers

How fast should a home service company respond to a new lead?

Within 5 minutes, and within 60 seconds if you can. Homeowners usually call more than one company, and the first one to respond has the best shot at booking the job.

How many times should I follow up with a lead?

More than most companies do. Follow up right away, later the same day, the next day, and then spaced out over the next couple of weeks, until they book, say no, or ask you to stop.

Should I call or text new leads?

Text first, then call if they ask. Most homeowners answer a text before they'll pick up a number they don't know.
