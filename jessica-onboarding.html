Day 1 in detail

What the intake actually asks.

Here it is in advance, so you can gather anything you don't have on hand. Most of it you already know by heart — it's the pricing rules that take people a minute.

Your services, in customer words

Not your internal names. What people type when they message you.

"AC not blowing cold" — not "diagnostic call-out"

Pricing rules

What she can say out loud, what's a range, and what has to wait for a human. This is the one that protects you.

"Can quote the $89 service call. Never quotes install."

Hours, service area, holidays

When you're open, how far you'll travel, and the days you're closed but leads still text.

She answers at 2am. She just won't promise you will.

Your calendar and its rules

Which calendar, how long a booking runs, travel buffers, and who owns which slot.

"90 min, 30 min drive buffer, never book Fridays after 3."

The ten questions you get every week

Your real FAQ, with the answers you'd give. This is the single biggest factor in how good she sounds.

"Do you take insurance?" "Are you licensed?" "How soon?"

Who you don't want

The leads that waste your day. She'll spot them and close them out politely instead of booking them.

Out of area, price shoppers, jobs you don't take

Escalation and handoff

Who she hands a live one to, how they get told, and how fast. Angry customers always go to a human.

"Text Mike within 5 minutes on anything over $5k."

Three texts that sound like you

Real messages you've sent a customer. She learns your voice from these, not from a tone slider.

Screenshots are fine. Copy and paste is fine.
Straight talk

Three things that stall a build.

These are the only reasons a seven-day launch has ever slipped for us. All three are avoidable if you know about them on day zero.

Undecided pricing rules

By far the most common. If you haven't settled what she's allowed to quote, she can't answer the question every lead opens with. Decide this before day one and nothing else slows you down.

Meta account access

Instagram and Messenger need a Business account with you as admin. If yours is a personal profile, that conversion takes a day or two on Meta's side, not ours. We'll launch text and web chat first and add socials when it clears.

No one to hand off to

Jessica books appointments and escalates live ones. If nobody is on the other end of an escalation, she's answering into a void. We need one name and one number before launch.
