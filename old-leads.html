Common questions

Straight answers

How do I get jobs from old estimates?

Follow up with a short, personal text that asks one easy question, then keep following up on a schedule. An AI employee like Infinity Reach Media's Dead List Miner can run this for every old estimate and past customer on your list.

Is it legal to text my old customers?

Texting people who gave you their number for your services is common, but rules vary, and opt-outs must be respected. We build opt-out handling in and review your list before launch. Ask your attorney if you're unsure about your situation.

How many contacts do I need?

Our 10-booked-appointments guarantee requires a list of at least 300 contacts with valid phone numbers. Smaller lists can still work, without the guarantee.

What's the guarantee?

If the Dead List Miner doesn't book 10 appointments from your existing list in the first 90 days, we keep running campaigns at no service fee until the tenth is booked. Full conditions are in our Terms of Service.
