Before you ask

The questions we get on every call.

What does "pilot" actually mean? Am I a guinea pig?

The agents are built and running, including on our own business. Pilot means we're still at twelve clients and still learning what breaks in the field. You get reduced rates and lock them for as long as you stay. We get honest feedback and, if it works, your permission to use the numbers. Nothing touches a real lead until you've tried to break it on test day.

Why include texts and calls instead of billing them through?

Because a bill you can't predict is a bill you resent. We'd rather quote one number and size the allowance with you honestly up front. If you're a heavy phone business we'll say so on the call rather than discover it in month three.

Can I start with The First 10 instead?

Yes, and plenty do. It's the paid trial on your existing list, and whatever you invest is credited toward your build fee if you continue. See The First 10 →

What happens when the twelve seats are full?

Pilot rates close for new clients and standard pricing starts. Anyone already in keeps their rate. We won't take your money and put you in a queue, so if we're full on the day you call, we'll tell you the real date we open up.

Is there a contract?

No. Month to month, cancel any time. The build fee is one-time and it's the part with the refund attached. Everything runs in accounts you own, so if you leave, you keep the number, the calendar, and every conversation.
