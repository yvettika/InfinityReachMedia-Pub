Common questions

Straight answers

How do I follow up with construction leads faster?

Put an AI employee on every lead source. It texts back within 60 seconds of a missed call or form, asks the qualifying questions your office would ask, and books the appointment. Infinity Reach Media sets this up for construction companies across the US in 7 days.

I already have an answering service. Why switch?

An answering service takes a message. An AI employee books the job. It replies in 60 seconds, qualifies the lead and puts the appointment on your calendar, then keeps following up if the person goes quiet.

Will the AI say something weird to my customers?

Approval mode is on from day one. The AI employee drafts, you approve with one tap. It tells people it's an AI assistant, and it hands anything complicated to a human.

What software is it built on?

GoHighLevel, Slack and Claude. We build inside your own accounts, so your data stays yours if you ever leave.

What does it cost?

It's priced on the leak it plugs, not the software. Book a free call and we'll price it against what you're currently losing, using your real numbers.

How do I follow up on construction bids without being pushy?

Use a set schedule with friendly, useful messages instead of 'just checking in.' An AI employee can run that schedule for every open bid, pass real questions to you, and stop the moment the client says no.
